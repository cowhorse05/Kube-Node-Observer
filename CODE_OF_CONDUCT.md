# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for everyone.

Project participants are expected to use inclusive language, accept constructive feedback, focus disagreements on technical decisions, and avoid harassment or disclosure of another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening or harmful.

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported privately through the repository's security advisory channel. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is based on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
