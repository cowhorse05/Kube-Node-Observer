KubeNode-Observer is installed as a privileged DaemonSet.

Check the rollout:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout status daemonset/{{ include "kubenode-observer.fullname" . }}

Forward the Agent API:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "kubenode-observer.fullname" . }} 18080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then run:
  kno doctor --endpoint http://127.0.0.1:18080
  kno inspect pod <pod> -n <namespace> --endpoint http://127.0.0.1:18080

Optional existing-stack integrations:
  --set serviceMonitor.enabled=true
  --set prometheusRule.enabled=true
  --set grafanaDashboard.enabled=true
